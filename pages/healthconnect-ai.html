Project Overview

HealthConnect AI is designed for students of Cebu Eastern College and authorized school clinic personnel. The proposed platform combines voluntary health tracking, health education, preventive reminders, and a permission-controlled clinic workflow to help students stay informed and facilitate appropriate follow-up.

♡

Health Tracking

Students can record personal wellness information and review their health-entry history.

✦

AI Wellness Tips

An AI component is planned to deliver general, non-diagnostic wellness education and preventive-care guidance.

◷

Preventive Reminders

Helpful reminders encourage health awareness and consistent everyday wellness practices.

Two Connected User Experiences

For Students

  • Account registration and clinic-approved access
  • Health profile, voluntary tracking, and entry history
  • General AI wellness tips and preventive reminders
  • Immediate safety guidance for serious warning criteria
  • Privacy and permission-based sharing controls

For Authorized Clinic Personnel

  • Review and approve applicable student registrations
  • Receive rule-triggered alerts for serious warning criteria
  • Review permitted records and coordinate follow-up
  • Document student wellness monitoring and follow-up
  • Generate approved health-monitoring reports

Safety-First Approach

Important distinction: HealthConnect AI is a proposed wellness support tool, not a medical diagnosis, prescription, or emergency response system. Clinic-approved rules — rather than AI-generated guesses — are intended to determine serious-warning alerts. Any real medical concern requires appropriate professional care.
